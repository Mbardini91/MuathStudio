About

A designer who also edits, animates, and speaks.

I'm Muath Albardini, a graphic designer and media producer based in Bahrain, and the founder of Muath Studio.

My work spans national-scale events, government campaigns, and local brands. Every project is designed bilingual from the first sketch, because in the Gulf the Arabic side is never an afterthought.

Clients come to me for the identity and stay for the motion, the edit, and the voice, since all of it is built from the same system.
